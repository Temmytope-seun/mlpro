Project Summary

This project uses a trained regression model to predict a student's math exam score from features such as gender, ethnicity, parental level of education, lunch type, test preparation course completion, and reading/writing scores. The model was trained on a student performance dataset using a scikit-learn preprocessing pipeline (imputation, scaling, one-hot encoding) and several candidate regressors (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, CatBoost, AdaBoost, and more), selected automatically based on validation R² score.

Problem Statement

Educators often lack early, data-driven signals to identify students who may be at risk of underperforming in exams. By surfacing a predicted score from readily available information, this tool aims to help teachers and administrators flag students who could benefit from additional support before final exams take place.
