Overview

What this project does

The model learns the relationship between a student's background, study preparation, and their reading/writing results — then predicts the math score they're likely to achieve.

Problem Statement

Educators often lack early, data-driven signals to identify students who may be at risk of underperforming in exams. By surfacing a predicted score from readily available information, this tool helps teachers and administrators flag students who could benefit from additional support before final exams take place.

Modeling Approach

Categorical features are one-hot encoded and numerical features are scaled inside a scikit-learn pipeline. Eight candidate regressors — including Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, CatBoost, and AdaBoost — are tuned with grid search, and the strongest performer on held-out data is automatically selected and deployed.

Pipeline

How it works

Four stages take raw data all the way to a live prediction served by this web app.

1
Data Ingestion

Raw student records are loaded and split into training and test sets.

2
Transformation

Numerical scores are scaled; categorical fields are imputed and one-hot encoded.

3
Model Selection

Multiple regressors are grid-searched and ranked by R² on the test set.

4
Deployment

The winning model is served through this Flask app for real-time predictions.
